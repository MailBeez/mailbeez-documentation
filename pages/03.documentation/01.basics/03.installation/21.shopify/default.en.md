Want to send a welcome email when someone registers in Shopify? The planned connector links your store to MailBeez.io, the hosted version of MailBeez. Start by asking MailBeez Services when your shop can connect.

## Request a connection

1. [Request a quote and consultation](https://www.mailbeez.com/about/contact). Share your Shopify store URL and the first emails you want to send, such as a welcome email after registration.
2. Discuss the features, prerequisites and timing for your store with MailBeez Services.
3. Once you accept the quote, MailBeez Services provides the connector and setup instructions. When your store is connected, you can [choose your first automation](/quickstart/step-2-choose-your-mailbeez).

See [compatibility for Shopify](/compatibility?platform=shopify) to check which modules you can use or configure in your hosted account. All stores using the hosted connection share the same feature set.
