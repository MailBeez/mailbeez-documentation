MailBeez Services can confirm which connector is planned for your Shopware version. The connector links your storefront to MailBeez.io, where you run your campaigns.

## Request a connection

1. [Request a quote and consultation](https://www.mailbeez.com/about/contact). Give your store address and specify whether you use Shopware 5 or Shopware 6. If you run several storefronts, say which one you want to connect first.
2. Discuss the features, prerequisites and timing for your store with MailBeez Services.
3. Once you accept the quote, MailBeez Services provides the connector and setup instructions. When your store is connected, you can [choose your first automation](/quickstart/step-2-choose-your-mailbeez).

See [compatibility for Shopware](/compatibility?platform=shopware) to check which modules you can use or configure in your hosted account. All stores using the hosted connection share the same feature set.
