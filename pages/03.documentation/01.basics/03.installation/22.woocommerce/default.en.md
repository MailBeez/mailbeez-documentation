A connector is planned to link your WooCommerce store to MailBeez.io. MailBeez Services can confirm availability for your WordPress installation during the consultation.

## Request a connection

1. [Request a quote and consultation](https://www.mailbeez.com/about/contact). Include the address of your WordPress store and your WooCommerce version. If an agency manages the store, include its technical contact.
2. Discuss the features, prerequisites and timing for your store with MailBeez Services.
3. Once you accept the quote, MailBeez Services provides the connector and setup instructions. When your store is connected, you can [choose your first automation](/quickstart/step-2-choose-your-mailbeez).

See [compatibility for WooCommerce](/compatibility?platform=woocommerce) to check which modules you can use or configure in your hosted account. All stores using the hosted connection share the same feature set.
