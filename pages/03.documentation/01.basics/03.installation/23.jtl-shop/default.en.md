To send a review invitation after a JTL-Shop order, ask MailBeez Services about the planned connection to MailBeez.io. The consultation is where you can confirm availability for your shop version and discuss that first campaign.

## Request a connection

1. [Request a quote and consultation](https://www.mailbeez.com/about/contact). Give the address and version of your JTL-Shop storefront. Describe a first use case, such as inviting recent buyers to review a product.
2. Discuss the features, prerequisites and timing for your store with MailBeez Services.
3. Once you accept the quote, MailBeez Services provides the connector and setup instructions. When your store is connected, you can [choose your first automation](/quickstart/step-2-choose-your-mailbeez).

See [compatibility for JTL-Shop](/compatibility?platform=jtl-shop) to check which modules you can use or configure in your hosted account. All stores using the hosted connection share the same feature set.
