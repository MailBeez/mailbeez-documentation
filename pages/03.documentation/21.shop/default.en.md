## What Shop reports tell you

Did more customers come back to buy this month? Open **Reports → Shop → Customers** and compare **Repeat buyers** with the previous period. If the card offers **View customers**, select it to see which accounts bought again.

When buyers mention damaged parcels, open **Reviews** if it’s available in your shop. Read the comments for the period when the parcels arrived. They can help you decide whether to change the packaging or investigate one delivery.
