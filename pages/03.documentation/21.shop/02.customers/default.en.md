How many customers ordered this month? How many had bought from you before? Open **Reports → Shop → Customers** to see both counts. A customer who registered last year and placed a second order this month counts as a repeat buyer this month.

## Read customer development

1. Select **Customer development**. Choose the reporting period and check the dates on the card. Keep the same customer group selected when comparing figures.
2. Read **New accounts** alongside **Purchasing customers**. The first counts registrations. The second counts customer accounts with a qualifying order during the period. A customer can register and order in different periods.
3. Compare **Repeat buyers** with the previous period. Each account with a second or later qualifying order counts once in the period total. If the card shows **View customers**, open it to see the matching accounts. For purchase patterns, choose **Buying behaviour**.

## Interpret a change

In this example, registrations fell from 16,364 to 12,668, a decrease of 22.6%. Before changing a welcome campaign, check whether **Purchasing customers** also fell. Registrations alone cannot tell you how many of those new accounts placed a first order.
