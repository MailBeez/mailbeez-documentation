You’ve introduced larger bags of coffee and want to know whether customers are coming back. Open **Reports → Shop → Overview**. On **Store insights**, **Customers** shows who bought again. If people mention that the new bags tear in transit, **Reviews** lets you read the feedback behind a rating.

Choose **Open view** on the card you need, then select the dates inside the report. **Reviews** is hidden in hosted accounts. In a self-hosted installation the available cards depend on the shop’s supported features.

For the results of an email campaign, follow **MailBeez results** at the bottom of the page.
