If your average rating drops, open **Reports → Shop → Reviews** and look at the number of reviews behind it. One disappointed buyer can move the average in a quiet week. Several comments about the same damaged item give you a specific product or parcel to check.

The **Reviews** report is hidden in hosted accounts. You can still [ask for product reviews](/mailbeez/review_advanced) if that automation is set up for your shop. Follow the steps below when the report is available.

1. In **Overview**, select the period you want to investigate. Read the average alongside the number of reviews.
2. In **Rating distribution**, select a star count to open the matching reviews. Read several before drawing a conclusion about a product or about service.
3. Use **Product reviews** for product feedback. If your shop also collects store reviews, open **Store reviews** to investigate the shopping experience separately.
