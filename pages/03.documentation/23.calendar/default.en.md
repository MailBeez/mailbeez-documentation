Check what else is scheduled before choosing a day for your newsletter. If Friday already has a sale announcement, look at the adjacent days too. The same subscribers could receive both mailings.

1. Open **Campaigns → Calendar** and choose **Week**, **Month**, **3 Months** or **Year**. Filter by **Newsletter** or **Automations** and go to your planned date.
2. Select a newsletter entry and check its details and sending status.
3. For changes to the mailing, open [Newsletter](/mailbeez/newsletter). After the send, use [Flows & newsletters](/mailbeez-results/flows-newsletters) to review the result.
