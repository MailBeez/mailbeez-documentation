[doc-callout type="note" title="Earlier MailBeez versions"]
To compare results across campaigns in V5, open [Flows & newsletters](/mailbeez-results/flows-newsletters). [Browse the legacy reference](/legacy).
[/doc-callout]

Set a target for the share of contacted buyers who go on to review a product. Compare the observed share on the gauge with your target. To change when buyers are invited, open the review module.

Check how many buyers received an invitation before interpreting a percentage change. With only a few recipients, a single review can noticeably move the gauge.
