[doc-callout type="note" title="Earlier MailBeez versions"]
To compare results across campaigns in V5, open [Flows & newsletters](/mailbeez-results/flows-newsletters). [Browse the legacy reference](/legacy).
[/doc-callout]

Check what share of the shoppers contacted by the no-purchase campaign placed a first order afterwards. Set a target percentage for that campaign and compare it with the observed result. For the V5 flow, see [Encourage a first order](/mailbeez/nopurchase_advanced).

Keep the number of contacted shoppers beside the percentage. Two first orders mean something different after ten invitations than after a thousand.
