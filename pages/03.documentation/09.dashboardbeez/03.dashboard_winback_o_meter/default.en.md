[doc-callout type="note" title="Earlier MailBeez versions"]
To compare results across campaigns in V5, open [Flows & newsletters](/mailbeez-results/flows-newsletters). [Browse the legacy reference](/legacy).
[/doc-callout]

Use the gauge to compare your winback target with the share of contacted customers who bought again. It counts purchases observed after contact. To inspect the timing and offer behind the result, see [Win back inactive customers](/mailbeez/winback_advanced).

Use the result to identify a period worth exploring. A later purchase from a contacted buyer does not establish how that buyer would have behaved without the message.
