Choose your shop under **Platform** and enter part of a name under **Find a module**. Open the matching guide for prerequisites. For example, a birthday flow that sends a personal coupon needs a working coupon system in the shop. **Reset filters** brings back the full list.

The settings under [System → Configuration](/configbeez) apply to every supported shop system. For hosted shops, **Settings hidden** means MailBeez Services handles configuration changes.

Shopify, WooCommerce, JTL-Shop and Shopware share one feature set through their planned hosted connections. Read the hosted entries as follows.

- **Supported**<br>
  A shop with a hosted connection can use the module. Open its guide to check any shop or plan requirements.
- **Settings hidden**<br>
  You cannot configure this module in a standard hosted account. It may still run in the background.
- **Unconfirmed**<br>
  Ask MailBeez Services whether the module covers your use case. The [installation guides](/basics/installation) explain how to arrange a consultation and get the connector.
