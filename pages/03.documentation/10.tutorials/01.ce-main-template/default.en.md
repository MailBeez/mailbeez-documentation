[doc-callout type="note" title="Earlier MailBeez versions"]
To change the shared email layout in V5, open [Main Templates](/content/main-templates). [Browse the legacy reference](/legacy).
[/doc-callout]

To change the shared header, footer or branding in the earlier editor, follow [Edit a main template](/tutorials/setting-up-main-template).

Use an email that already shows the shared header as your example. After the edit, preview another campaign that uses the same template too.
