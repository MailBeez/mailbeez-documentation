To see how people interact with campaign emails, decide which opens and clicks MailBeez should record. A report can only show events collected while the corresponding tracking was enabled.

An open or a click is a recorded interaction with a message. To investigate visits on the shop website after a click, check the campaign parameters used by your analytics service.

Open **System → Configuration** and select **Open & Click Tracking**.

Read the recorded figures under **Reports → MailBeez → Email performance**. Compare periods with the same tracking settings.
