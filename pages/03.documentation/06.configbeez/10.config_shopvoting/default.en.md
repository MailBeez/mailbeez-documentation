A buyer orders six wine glasses. “Everything arrived in one piece, and you replaced a missing gift card the next day” would help another shopper decide whether to order a gift from you. A store review gives that feedback a home. For comments about the weight or shape of a glass, use [product reviews](/mailbeez/review_advanced).

## Collect feedback about the shopping experience

Open **System → Extensions → Add-ons → Feedback and analytics** and select **Collect reviews in your store**.

[doc-steps]

[doc-step]
**Check where buyers leave a review**

Open your shop’s review form as a customer. Check that it identifies your store and asks about the shopping experience. The form, the invitation and the display of published reviews each have their own setup.
[/doc-step]

[doc-step]
**Find the feedback in MailBeez**

Open **Manage reviews** and look for a review you recognize by its date and wording. To decide which buyers receive an invitation and when it goes out, use the [store review flow](/mailbeez/shoprating_advanced).
[/doc-step]

[/doc-steps]
