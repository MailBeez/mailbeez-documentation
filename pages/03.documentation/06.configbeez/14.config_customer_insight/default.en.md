A florist phones about a delivery of vases. Two customers have the same surname, so support starts with the email address on the order. The Customer Insight panel brings the available customer information into the shop admin while the team works on the enquiry.

## Make the panel available where support works

Open **System → Extensions → Add-ons → Customer data and content** and select **Show customer details in the store admin**.

Open the order in the shop admin with the role your support team uses. Open the customer panel and compare the address shown there with the enquiry. Which information appears depends on the shop integration, access rights and installed modules.

If the tab is missing on one admin page, check **Exclude admin pages**. If it covers a control your team needs, adjust **Panel tab offset** and reopen the same order to check its position.
