Open **System → Configuration → Preview & Test Data** to choose the customer or order used in an email preview. To test a product list, use an order with two different items. You’ll spot a missing or repeated product more easily.

1. Set **Sample first name** and **Sample last name** to check a greeting or subject line.
2. For content that depends on a shop record, check **Preview customer ID** and **Preview order ID**. Use a suitable test record with the details you need, such as an order with several products.
3. Reopen the email preview and check the greeting, order details and links. To control where test mail is delivered, open [Simulation Mode](/configbeez/config_simulation).
