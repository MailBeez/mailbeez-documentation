Check what happens when a subscriber follows an unsubscribe link. Open **System → Configuration → Anti-Spam & Consent** and review the subscription requirement and the scope of a block. Test one module-level opt-out and check whether other modules can still send to that test address.

Use the invitation email itself for the test. The wording beside its unsubscribe link is what tells the recipient whether they are stopping one type of message or all mail.
