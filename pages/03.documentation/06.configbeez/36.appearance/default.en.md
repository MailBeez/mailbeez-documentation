Adjust the workspace for the screen you use to manage the shop. Open **System → Configuration → Appearance**.

Use a screen you work with every day to choose the density. A long customer list makes spacing differences easier to judge than a nearly empty page.

1. Choose **System** to follow your device, **Light** for a light background or **Dark** for a dark background. The color choice applies in this browser.
2. Under **Interface density**, choose how much space you want between items. Use **Compact** when you want to see more rows at once.
3. Use **Save settings** for your display preferences. Check a table you use often to see how it reads.
