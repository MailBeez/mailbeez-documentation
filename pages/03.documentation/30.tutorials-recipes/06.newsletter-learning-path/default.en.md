Your spring collection launches on Friday. You want to email confirmed subscribers who do not yet have a shop account. Follow this path to choose the audience, check the mailing and compare its clicks with an earlier edition.

You’ll need access to the Newsletter module, two known contacts to check your audience, the collection link and a test inbox. You can use the worked figures for the final step.

## 1. Who should get the invitation?

Open [Lists and Segments](/contacts/lists-segments). Check your selection against a confirmed subscriber without a shop account and an existing shop customer. Can you explain why only the first address belongs in this list?

## 2. Is Friday’s edition ready?

Follow [Plan a newsletter for Friday](/tutorials-and-recipes/plan-a-newsletter). Use the audience you checked, open the test email on your phone and tap the link. You’ve finished preparing the edition when the right collection opens and you can find its time and status in the calendar.

## 3. Why did one edition get fewer clicks?

After sending, follow [Investigate fewer newsletter clicks](/tutorials-and-recipes/investigate-newsletter-clicks) to compare two similar editions. If your results are still coming in, use the tutorial’s example. By the end, explain how clicks can halve while clicks as a share of recorded opens stay the same. Which figure would you check next?
