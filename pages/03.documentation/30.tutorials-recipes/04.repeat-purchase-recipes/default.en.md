Start with a product you can name and a reason its buyer would want to hear from you. In [Custom Flows](/mailbeez/custom-flows), the audience decides who gets the message. These three recipes use the last purchase of a particular product as their starting point. Adjust the example intervals to match your customers’ buying habits.

## Coffee refills

A customer who drinks your house espresso may use a one-kilo bag in about a month. To offer a refill, create an audience of subscribed buyers whose **last purchase of that bag** was 28 to 35 days ago. Choose the order statuses that count as completed purchases in your shop.

Link to the same beans and bag size. Check the list with two regulars. Someone who last bought the bag 30 days ago should match. Someone who bought it 30 days ago and again last week should be too early. If you want to remind anyone who has stopped shopping with you, use [Win back inactive customers](/mailbeez/winback_advanced).

## Replacement filters

Suppose the manufacturer recommends replacing a machine’s water filter after six months. Select buyers of that **exact machine model** whose last matching purchase was 175 to 185 days ago. Exclude buyers of the matching replacement filter. Both product rules use the order statuses you select.

Link to the correct filter and tell the reader where to find the model number on the machine. Check an owner who bought beans last week and another who already bought the replacement filter. Only the first should appear. This recipe covers the first replacement. A later replacement would need its own timing based on the filter purchase.

## Compatible accessories

A scale can help a new pour-over brewer owner repeat a cup they enjoyed. Select subscribed customers who bought that brewer two to three weeks ago. Exclude buyers of the scale you plan to recommend. Verify that the scale fits the brewer you feature.

In the email, show one use for the scale, such as weighing the water for a second cup. Check an order that included both the brewer and the scale. Its buyer should be absent. If your brewing course already recommends the same scale, include the tip there and skip the separate email.
