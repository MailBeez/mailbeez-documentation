Your spring collection opens on Friday. You want to invite newsletter subscribers who have no shop account yet. Have the collection URL, access to the Newsletter module and a test inbox ready.

[doc-steps]
[doc-step]
**Check who should receive the invitation**

In [Contacts → Lists and Segments](/contacts/lists-segments), choose **New List** and select prospects. Set the rules for confirmed subscribers and name the list so you can find it in the newsletter. Check a known subscribed prospect and an existing shop customer. Only the prospect belongs in this audience.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Prepare the Friday edition**

Open [Campaigns → Newsletter](/mailbeez/newsletter) and select **New Newsletter**. In **Audience**, select your list. Choose a layout under **Template** and set the planned sending time under **Details**. If you started from the list, the audience may already be selected.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Follow the link as a subscriber**

Send a test email to yourself. Open it on your phone, tap the main link and check that it opens the spring collection. If subscribers read a second language, test that version and its destination before marking the newsletter ready to send.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Find the mailing before and after Friday**

In [Campaigns → Calendar](/calendar), select **Week** and filter by **Newsletter**. Open Friday’s entry and check the time and sending status. After processing, select **Newsletters** under [Reports → MailBeez → Flows & newsletters](/mailbeez-results/flows-newsletters) and look up this mailing. The final audience can change as people subscribe or opt out.
[/doc-step]
[/doc-steps]

After sending, [compare clicks across two editions](/tutorials-and-recipes/investigate-newsletter-clicks). For the full sequence, open the [newsletter learning path](/tutorials-and-recipes/newsletter-learning-path).
