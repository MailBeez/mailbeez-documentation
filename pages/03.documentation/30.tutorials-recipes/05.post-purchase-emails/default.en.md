The first question after buying a pour-over brewer is often “How much coffee do I use?” A week later, the same buyer may wonder why their coffee tastes bitter. Two short emails let them try the first lesson before you send the second. For a single task, such as cleaning a grinder, one email can be enough.

## First-brew course

[doc-steps]
[doc-step]
**Start with buyers of the brewer**

In [Contacts → Lists and Segments](/contacts/lists-segments), select subscribed buyers of the pour-over brewer. Use the product and current order statuses that identify a completed purchase. Choose a purchase-age window that allows time for delivery. Check one brewer buyer and one customer who bought only beans.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Write the first lesson**

Create a [Custom Flow](/mailbeez/custom-flows) for the saved audience. Open with the question on the buyer’s mind. Give the dose and water quantity from your brewer’s recipe, then link to the full instructions. Send yourself a test and follow the link on your phone.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Give the buyer time to try it**

From the first email, open the follow-up options. Choose **Next email**, set **Days** to 7 and select **Create draft**. This delay starts when the first email is sent to that person. Write the second lesson around one adjustment, such as changing the grind for a bitter cup. Review both drafts and their activation settings before starting the course.
[/doc-step]
[/doc-steps]

## Grinder care

For a cleaning reminder, create a separate audience of subscribed buyers of the grinder model you cover. Use the care interval in its manual to choose when owners should first hear from you. If the first cleaning is due after about a month, check one buyer from a month ago and another from yesterday. Only the first should qualify.

Write one email that names the model and links to its cleaning instructions. Check that the link opens the correct manual. If these buyers already get a lesson on cleaning this grinder, put the instructions in that lesson.
