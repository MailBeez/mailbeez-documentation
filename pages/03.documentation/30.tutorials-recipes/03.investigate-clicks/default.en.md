Your last newsletter recorded 30 clicks. The previous one had 60. Before changing the offer, find out whether fewer people opened the email or fewer of those readers clicked.

[doc-steps]
[doc-step]
**Find comparable mailings**

Open [Reports → MailBeez → Flows & newsletters](/mailbeez-results/flows-newsletters) and choose **Newsletters**. Select each newsletter by name. If it is missing from the short list, open **Full leaderboard**. Record the send date and the counts for sends, recorded opens and clicks for each edition. Compare mailings with similar audiences and allow roughly the same time for readers to respond. A newsletter sent an hour ago has had little time to collect clicks.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Read the denominator**

Open [Reports → MailBeez → Email performance](/mailbeez-results/email-performance). The **Beez-O-Graph** expresses clicks as a percentage of recorded opens. The graph covers the selected period, so where several campaigns overlap, use the individual mailing figures for your comparison.

Suppose the older mailing had 600 recorded opens and 60 clicks. That is 10%. The newer one had 300 opens and 30 clicks, also 10%. The drop in clicks already has an explanation in the smaller number of recorded opens. Check the send counts to see whether the audience was smaller too.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Choose one thing to investigate**

If both editions instead had 600 recorded opens, 30 clicks would be 5%. Open the newer email on a phone and try its main link. Check the offer, its position and the destination page. If the link opens the wrong collection, you have a specific correction for the next edition.
[/doc-step]
[/doc-steps]
