Under **System → Extensions → External services**, **All** shows the connections installed in your shop. Choose the provider for the task at hand. The categories depend on the services installed.

External service settings are hidden in a hosted account. If you want to import reviews, for example, ask MailBeez Services which connection is available for your shop.

If your account gives you access to the installation catalog, choose **Add integration** to see what is available for your shop.
