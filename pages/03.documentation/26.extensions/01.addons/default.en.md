Under **System → Extensions → Add-ons**, **All** shows the add-ons installed in your shop. To send a coupon only to retail customers, open **Filters**. To include an invoice number in the email, look under **Customer data and content**. The categories you see depend on which add-ons are installed.

In a hosted account, you can manage [sending windows](/configbeez/config_cron_advanced), [customer blocks](/filterbeez/filter_check_block_all) and [customer groups](/filterbeez/filter_check_group). The other add-on settings are hidden.

If your account gives you access to the installation catalog, choose **Add add-on** to see what is available for your shop.
