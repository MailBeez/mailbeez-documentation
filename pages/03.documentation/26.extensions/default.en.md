Suppose you want to send a refill coupon to retail buyers while keeping wholesale accounts out of that mailing. Under **System → Extensions → Add-ons**, a customer group filter can narrow who receives it. To bring Trusted Shops reviews into your store, open **External services → Integrations** and choose your provider.

The planned hosted connections for Shopify, WooCommerce, JTL-Shop and Shopware give merchants access to a smaller set of controls. Check [Compatibility](/compatibility) to see which modules you can manage.
