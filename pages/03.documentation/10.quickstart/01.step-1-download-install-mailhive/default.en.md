[doc-steps]
[doc-step]
**Choose your installation guide**

If MailBeez is already connected to your shop, go straight to step 3. Otherwise, find your platform under [Installation](/basics/installation).

Hosted connections are planned for [Shopify](/basics/installation/shopify), [WooCommerce](/basics/installation/woocommerce), [JTL-Shop](/basics/installation/jtl-shop) and [Shopware](/basics/installation/shopware). Use your platform guide to request a consultation and quote. MailBeez Services confirms availability and supplies the connector once you accept the quote.

If MailBeez runs inside your shop, have the exact shop version ready and choose its installation guide.

[doc-step]
**Install or activate MailBeez**

For a hosted connection, follow the setup instructions from MailBeez Services.

[GambioCloud](/basics/installation/basic-installation-gambiocloud) includes the integration. Activate **MailBeez** under **Modules → Modules-Center** and follow the installer.

For [Zen Cart 2.x](/basics/installation/basic-installation-zen-cart-2-x), copy the files into the shop first, then start the installer from the shop administration.
[/doc-step]
[doc-step]
**Open the dashboard**

Once setup is complete, open MailBeez from your shop or use the access details from MailBeez Services. When you see the **Dashboard**, you can [choose your first automation](/quickstart/step-2-choose-your-mailbeez).
[/doc-step]
[/doc-steps]
