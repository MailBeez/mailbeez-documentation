## What happened after a send?

Did you send fewer emails this month, or did MailBeez record fewer clicks on a similar number of sends? Under **Reports → MailBeez**, open **Email performance** for the totals. Use **Flows & newsletters** to see which mailings contributed to the change. If the audience for your next newsletter is smaller than expected, check **Contacts** for signups and opt-outs.
