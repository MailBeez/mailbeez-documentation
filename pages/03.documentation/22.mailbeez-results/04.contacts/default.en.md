If fewer people can receive your next newsletter, open **Reports → MailBeez → Contacts**. Check whether fewer people signed up or more opted out. Use the same dates for both figures, then check the audience selected for that mailing.

- **Customers**<br>
  See the figures for customer accounts. Check the date beside **Current contacts** to see when that snapshot was taken.
- **Prospects**<br>
  See how newsletter signups without a customer account develop. Use the period selector to investigate changes around a signup campaign.
- **Opt-outs & blocks**<br>
  Check why an address may be excluded from sending.

A stored account and a contact eligible for a particular newsletter are different counts. Before scheduling a mailing, check the selected audience under [Lists and Segments](/contacts/lists-segments).
