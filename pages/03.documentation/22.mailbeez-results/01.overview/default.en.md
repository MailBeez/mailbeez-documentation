Your Friday newsletter offered a tasting box. On Monday you want to know how the mailing performed alongside the reorder reminders that also ran over the weekend. Open **Reports → MailBeez → Overview**. On **MailBeez results**, choose email performance for the totals, or the individual mailing to see what the newsletter contributed. Include the dates the emails actually went out.
