Find which flow or newsletter accounts for a change in email activity. Open **Reports → MailBeez → Flows & newsletters** before deciding which campaign to edit.

Compare campaigns with similar purposes. A scheduled announcement may reach a large audience in one day, while a cart reminder reaches people individually as their carts qualify.

1. Choose **Flows** for automated sequences or **Newsletters** for individual mailings.
2. Select a period that includes the activity you want to compare. Check how many messages were sent before comparing clicks or attributed revenue. A daily flow has more opportunities to send than a one-off newsletter.
3. Once you have identified a campaign, open [Automations](/mailbeez) or [Newsletter](/mailbeez/newsletter) to review its audience, timing and content.
