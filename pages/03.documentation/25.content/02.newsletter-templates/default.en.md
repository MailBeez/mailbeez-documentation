Choose a newsletter layout around what you want readers to do. To introduce one new product, look for room for a photo and a link to its shop page. To present a collection, start with a layout that can show several items.

1. Open **Content → Newsletter Templates → Gallery**. Use **Modern** or **Responsive** to narrow the selection.
2. Select **Preview** on a design and check whether it has room for your headline, products and main link.
3. When creating the [Newsletter](/mailbeez/newsletter), choose the design in **Template** and preview it again with your own content. For a shared header or footer, use [Main Templates](/content/main-templates).
