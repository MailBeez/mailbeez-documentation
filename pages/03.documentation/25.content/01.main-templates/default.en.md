To change a logo or footer in several emails, find the main template those campaigns share. Preview two messages that use it. A wide logo can look fine above a short greeting and crowd the products in a newsletter.

1. Open **Content → Main Templates → Main Designs**. Choose the design your campaign uses and select **Preview**.
2. Choose **Edit** to update the shared layout. Check the logo, header links and company details in each language your shop sends.
3. Preview an email from a campaign that uses the design before applying it across campaigns. For a newsletter’s starting layout, use [Newsletter Templates](/content/newsletter-templates).
