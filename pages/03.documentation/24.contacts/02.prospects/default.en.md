When someone asks about a newsletter confirmation but has no shop account, search for the address they entered on the signup form. Their prospect record shows whether they confirmed their subscription.

1. Open **Contacts → Prospects** and search by name, email address or prospect ID. Use the status and source filters to narrow the results.
2. Check the record’s confirmation status before selecting the address for a newsletter. If the person has become a customer, also check [Customers](/contacts/customers).
3. If no record exists, use **New Prospect** for one person or **Import** for a file. Check the source and subscription status of the addresses you add.
