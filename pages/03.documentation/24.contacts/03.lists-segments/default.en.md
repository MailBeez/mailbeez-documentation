Suppose you want to email confirmed newsletter subscribers who have yet to open a shop account. Build a list of prospects. Keep two addresses handy to check the result. The subscribed prospect should appear in your list. The existing shop customer should be absent.

1. Open **Contacts → Lists and Segments → New List** and choose the type of contacts you want to include.
2. Set the criteria for the mailing. Check a few matching records and the recipient count before using the list.
3. Select the list as the **Audience** for the newsletter.

MailBeez refreshes the selection before sending. The number can change as contacts qualify, unsubscribe or become blocked.
