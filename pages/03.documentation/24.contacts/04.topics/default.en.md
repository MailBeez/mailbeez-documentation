Give shoppers a choice they’ll recognize on the signup form, such as “New hiking gear”. Make it a public topic if subscribers should be able to choose it themselves. For a grouping that only your team uses, choose an internal segment.

1. Open **Contacts → Topics**. Choose **Topics** for an interest subscribers can select. Choose **Internal Segments** for a grouping your team uses internally.
2. To add a public interest, choose **New Topic**. Give it a name subscribers will recognize and check how it appears in your signup form.
3. To select recipients for a mailing, open [Lists and Segments](/contacts/lists-segments).
