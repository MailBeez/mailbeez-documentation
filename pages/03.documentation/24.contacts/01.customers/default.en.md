When a buyer asks about a particular order or email, start with their customer profile. Have the order date or approximate send time ready.

Two people can share a surname, and one person may have used different email addresses. Match the order reference or address before interpreting a customer’s history.

1. Open **Contacts → Customers** and search for the customer. Open the matching profile.
2. Check the available purchase and communication history. Match the order or message to the date in the customer’s question.
3. If a particular email is missing, look up the recipient under [Sending & status](/sending-status). For trends across all buyers, use the [Customers report](/shop/customers).
