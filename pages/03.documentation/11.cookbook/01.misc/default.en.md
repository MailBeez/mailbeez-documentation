Two details from a shop account can help you choose whom to contact. The customer group tells you who belongs to a group today. A recorded date of birth lets MailBeez find customers whose birthday is approaching.

## Contact members of a customer group

Suppose the shop has moved several retailers into its wholesale group. In the earlier newsletter list editor, open **Edit Segmentation** on a list of subscribed customers. Under **Groups**, choose the wholesale group. Save and use **Test Segmentation** with one wholesale account and one retail account.

Both existing and newly added wholesale accounts can match. If you want to announce a particular change only to the retailers who just moved, compare the list with the accounts changed in the shop before preparing the mailing. The group filter alone has no date for that move.

## Invite customers to add a birthday

Check first that customers can save their date of birth in your shop account form. Open that form using a test account without a birthday, enter a date and check that it remains there after signing in again.

You can link to this form in a newsletter for subscribers who wish to receive a birthday greeting. Use the account page from your own storefront. The [birthday flow](/mailbeez/coupon_birthday) can use the date once it is available in the shop. An account with no date cannot qualify for a birthday greeting.
